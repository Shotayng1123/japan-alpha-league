Practice simulation — not actual trading or investment advice. Every book starts as an MSCI Japan neutral-weight replication (~¥1tn); alpha comes from trading away from it. Order size can be given in ¥bn, shares, or bps of NAV (converted at the first fill's close). Long-only: sell down to zero to express underweights (max UW per name = its benchmark weight). Fills at TSE closes with a 20%-of-ADV daily participation cap. DVA = active weight × (stock return − benchmark return), i.e. each name's contribution to today's excess return. Prices: Yahoo Finance (delayed). Benchmark: MSCI Japan Net Total Return, JPY (official EOD levels). Index weights and sectors via iShares MSCI Japan ETF (EWJ) published holdings as an MSCI Japan proxy. P/E FY-E = price over company-guidance EPS for the current fiscal year (Yahoo! Finance Japan); consensus FY+1 P/E is not freely available.
